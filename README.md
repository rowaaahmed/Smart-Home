This project represents a smart home automation prototype with a strong emphasis on sustainability and efficient energy management. The system utilized a solar panel tracking mechanism as a renewable energy source to
support home lighting and battery charging, demonstrating the practical importance of solar energy in reducing reliance on conventional power. Intelligent automation and safety were achieved through embedded control,
including RFID-based access control, sensor-driven garage operation, and an electromechanical elevator system. To enhance user awareness and security, Blink software was integrated to send real-time mobile 
notifications in the event of a laser-based security breach or smoke sensor detection. The project combined renewable energy integration, sensor fusion, and electromechanical design to present a scalable model for
sustainable smart living.
